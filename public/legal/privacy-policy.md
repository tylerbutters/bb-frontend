## Privacy Laws

Bunsho Builder is based in New Zealand and aims to handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 2020. If you use Bunsho Builder from outside New Zealand, local privacy laws may also apply.

For users in the European Union, we process personal data under the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) (the "GDPR"). For users in the United Kingdom, we process personal data under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer because we do not believe Bunsho Builder falls within the categories of controllers or processors required to appoint one under Article 37 of the GDPR. Privacy questions can be sent to support@bunshobuilder.com.

## Legal Basis For Processing

For users in the EU and UK, we collect and process personal data only when we have a legal basis under Article 6 of the GDPR or UK GDPR.

We rely on the following legal bases:

- **Contract**: processing is necessary to create and manage accounts, authenticate users, provide challenge checking, translations, stats, history, quota features, account settings, and account deletion.
- **Legitimate interests**: processing is necessary to protect Bunsho Builder from abuse, fraud, spam, and security threats; maintain service reliability; debug errors; enforce quotas and rate limits; respond to support requests; and improve the service in ways that do not override users' rights and freedoms.
- **Legal obligation**: processing is necessary where we must comply with applicable laws, respond to valid legal requests, handle privacy requests, keep required records, or manage security or privacy incidents.

If you do not provide the personal information needed for account-based features, we may not be able to create or manage your account or provide features such as login, challenge checking, saved stats and history, quota tracking, and account settings.

If we are legally required to collect, keep, use, or disclose certain personal information, we may be unable to provide the service, respond to requests, comply with legal obligations, or continue providing access to an account if the required information is not provided.

## How We Protect Personal Information

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal information. Bunsho Builder uses HTTPS encryption in transit, secure HTTP-only session cookies, hashed passwords, hashed session tokens, and hashed reset or confirmation codes. We also use rate limiting, request size limits, CORS/origin checks, and access controls to reduce unauthorised access and abuse.

User data is stored with our hosting and database providers, and access is limited to people who need it to operate, maintain, or support Bunsho Builder. We do not intentionally log passwords, session cookies, reset codes, or confirmation codes. Where we use third-party providers, such as email, AI, translation, hosting, or database services, we choose providers that support secure handling of data.

No online service can guarantee complete security, but we work to keep the amount of personal information we collect limited, protect it appropriately, and respond promptly if we become aware of a security issue.

You are responsible for using a strong password, keeping your login details safe, and contacting us if you suspect unauthorised access.

## Cookies And Local Storage

Bunsho Builder uses a first-party session cookie named `bb_session` to keep users logged in and protect account access. This cookie is necessary for the service to work and is not used for advertising or analytics. In production, this cookie is intended to be `HttpOnly`, `Secure`, and `SameSite=Lax`.

Bunsho Builder also uses browser local storage for functionality and convenience. Local storage may include:

- `bbCurrentUser`, which stores limited current-user display state for the frontend;
- `bbLocalGameResults:[user id]`, which stores local game results and history fallback data;
- `bbFreeGameLimitIntroDismissed:[user id]`, which stores whether a free-limit notice has been dismissed; and
- user preferences, such as default feedback visibility.

You can clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Clearing them may log you out, remove local-only history, or reset local preferences.

We do not currently use analytics cookies, targeting cookies, advertising cookies, third-party cookies, advertising pixels, or marketing tracking. If we add these in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and request consent where required.
